# 📱 Motorola Sales & Performance Data Analytics Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, volume distribution, transaction methods, and customer feedback trends across major Indian cities.
---
## 📊 Dashboard Preview
![Motorola Sales Dashboard](images/dashboard_preview.png)
---
## 🔑 Key Insights & Metrics
* **Total Revenue Generated:** $769M across 4K transactions.
* **Volume Sold:** ~19K units with an average transaction value of ₹40K.
* **Geographic Distribution:** Covers major tier-1 & tier-2 hubs (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad).
* **Customer Satisfaction:** Majority rating lies at 5 stars (1,488 customers) followed by 4 stars (843 customers).
* **Top Revenue Days:** Peak sales recorded on Saturdays ($115M) and Mondays ($114M).
* **Payment Preference:** Debit Cards (29.44%) and Credit Cards (25.57%) account for over 50% of payment channels.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI
